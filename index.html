Wildlife Poaching Hotspot Predictor

AI-powered risk maps to protect endangered species

About the Project

Using historical poaching incident records combined with geospatial features such as proximity to roads, water sources, park boundaries, vegetation density, and seasonal patterns, our model predicts which grid cells are at elevated poaching risk in the coming month.

Risk Zones

High Risk
Immediate patrol priority
Medium Risk
Regular monitoring
Low Risk
Routine checks

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Collect historical poaching incident data.
  2. Integrate geospatial features (roads, water, vegetation, boundaries).
  3. Train ML model to predict risk zones.
  4. Evaluate with precision and recall metrics.
  5. Generate risk heatmap overlay on protected area.
  6. Recommend patrol prioritization strategy.
